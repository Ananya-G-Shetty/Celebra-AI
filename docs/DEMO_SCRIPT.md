# Celebra AI — 3-5 Minute Video Demonstration Script
### Google Build with AI • PromptWars Challenge 5: Celebration & Event Planner

**Presenter**: Smitha  
**Target Duration**: 3 to 4 minutes  

---

### [00:00 – 00:30] 1. Introduction & The Challenge
- **Visual**: Landing Page (`http://localhost:3000`).
- **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Hello everyone! I'm Smitha, and this is **Celebra AI** — built for Google Build with AI PromptWars Challenge 5. Our tagline is 'Every celebration deserves a perfect plan.'  
  > In India, planning a wedding, birthday, or community utsav involves intricate cultural customs, strict budgets in Indian Rupees (₹), and sudden disruptions like monsoon rain or vendor cancellations.  
  > Celebra AI is not a generic chatbot that generates text essays. It unites Google Gemini 1.5 structured JSON intelligence with deterministic financial math and rapid emergency contingency workflows."*

---

### [00:30 – 01:15] 2. Event Onboarding & Multi-Step Wizard
- **Visual**: Click **"Create Your Event"** to enter `/plan`.
- **Action**:
  - Show Step 1: Select "Wedding & Muhurtham", enter name *"Smitha & Karthik's South Indian Wedding"*, Bangalore location, 350 guests.
  - Show Step 2: Set Budget to ₹15,00,000 with Moderate flexibility; highlight Catering, Photography, and Venue priorities.
  - Show Step 3: Highlight Indian cultural nuances: *"Authentic Karnataka Banana-Leaf Sadhya, Nadaswaram troupe, eco-friendly brass diyas"*.
  - Show Step 4: Review summary and click **"Generate AI Celebration Blueprint"**.
  - Show progressive generation state, followed by celebratory confetti bursting!
- **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Our 4-step onboarding wizard collects core details, budget in INR, and cultural traditions. When we click Generate, Google Gemini 1.5 synthesizes a 7-dimensional master plan validated strictly via JSON schema."*

---

### [01:15 – 02:00] 3. Master Dashboard & Structured AI Plan
- **Visual**: Master Celebration Dashboard (`/dashboard`).
- **Action**:
  - Point out the countdown banner (days remaining, venue, budget pill).
  - Walk through the 7 AI sections:
    1. Creative Concept & Approach
    2. Theme, Stage Decor & Marigold/Jasmine Color Palette
    3. Catering Highlights (Sadhya menu, live Obbattu station, Sattvic dietary split)
    4. Entertainment & Logistics (valet, elder wheelchair access)
    5. Category-wise expense distribution in INR
    6. Market estimate pricing notice.
- **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Here on the dashboard, Gemini has synthesized our complete event roadmap. Notice how catering separates pure vegetarian and Sattvic counters, and logistics accounts for elder accessibility and valet parking in Bangalore."*

---

### [02:00 – 02:45] 4. Deterministic Budgeting & AI Budget Optimizer
- **Visual**: Navigate to **Budget Planner** (`/dashboard/budget`).
- **Action**:
  - Show the Recharts Donut Pie Chart and Estimated vs. Actual Spend Bar Chart.
  - Point out that financial arithmetic (totals, actuals, remaining buffer) is deterministic.
  - Open **"AI Budget Optimizer"**.
  - Type: *"Save ₹50,000 without compromising Catering & Photography."*
  - Run optimizer: show Gemini's strategic trade-offs (switching imported flowers to local Mysore marigolds, digital WhatsApp invites).
  - Click **"Apply Reallocations to Budget"**.
- **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Under the hood, all financial calculations are deterministic pure TypeScript — zero hallucinated math.  
  > But when we need savings, our AI Budget Optimizer analyzes trade-offs without touching our protected priorities, strictly staying below our specified limit."*

---

### [02:45 – 03:30] 5. Timeline, Vendors & Flagship Emergency Planner
- **Visual**:
  - Quick glance at **Timeline** (`/dashboard/timeline`): 6 milestone phases from 30 days before to Event Day.
  - Quick glance at **Vendors** (`/dashboard/vendors`): Click *"AI Vetting Questions"* to show hidden GST tax and overtime checks.
  - Navigate to **Emergency Contingency Room** (`/dashboard/emergency`).
- **Action**:
  - Select preset: *"Sudden Monsoon Rainfall on Outdoor Lawn"*.
  - Click **"Analyze Contingency Strategy & Triage Plan"**.
  - Highlight Gemini's situational assessment: Severity rating, affected tasks, 3 vetted alternatives, and instant triage checklist.
  - Point out the user confirmation gate: click *"Apply Urgent Tasks to My Timeline"*.
- **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Our flagship differentiator is the Smart Emergency Planner. When unexpected rain threatens our 350-guest lawn reception, Gemini analyzes the blast radius, suggests covered pavilion options, and drafts triage action items — with explicit user confirmation before touching the timeline."*
